# Making Sense of the Score
## Connecting Research and Clinical Practice for Measurement-Based Care
Repository for October 1st, 2026, Nationwide Children's Hospital Grand Rounds Presentation
Presenters: Musa Yilanli, MD, Courtney Cleminshaw-Mahan, PhD, and Ellie Nienaber, BA

Objectives:
1. Understand the connection between research and practice relative to measurement-based care (MBC) in behavioral health.
2. Formulate questions related to the implementation of MBC for a multidisciplinary panel from across the hospital.
3. Incorporate measurement findings, multiple perspectives, and functional goals into clinical decision making.
4. Explain how specific measures can be used in different stages of MBC.

Some of the information cited in this presentation is currently under peer review. Updated citations will be added to this repository's wiki page as they become available.
